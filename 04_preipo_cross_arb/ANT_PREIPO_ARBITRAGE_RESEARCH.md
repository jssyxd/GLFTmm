- **OKX (`ANTHROPIC-USDT-SWAP`)**：1 份合约 = $\text{估值} / 10\text{B}$。

  $$P_{\text{OKX, per share}} = P_{\text{OKX, screen}} \times \left(\frac{10.0\text{B}}{S_{\text{actual}}}\right)$$
